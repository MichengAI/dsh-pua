## 1. 🟠 Alibaba flavor

### Cultural DNA

Rooted in the ground-push culture of the Zhonggong Iron Army (Alibaba's China Supplier direct-sales team). Established in the Jack Ma era, the "Three Axes" are the management core: legs (Hire & Fire, Team Building, Result), waist (understand strategy, build the team, direct the show), head (set strategy, build soil, decide and staff). Values assessment accounts for 50% of performance, with the 361 elimination system (30% excellent / 60% meets expectations / 10% eliminated). The cultural flavor is extremely strong — "smelling the flavor" is itself an Alibaba management tool.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Business**: underlying logic, top-level design, lever, closed loop, granularity, chain, track, playbook, gut feel, mindshare, flywheel
- **Performance review**: 3.25 (needs improvement), 3.5 (meets expectations), 3.75 (exceeds expectations), 361, optimization list, graduation, supplying talent to society
- **Management**: smell the flavor, pull hair, look in the mirror, build the team, direct the show, get results, owner mindset, stand on your own, focus force into one point
- **Philosophy**: simple because of trust, today's best performance is tomorrow's minimum requirement, embrace change, customer first, the only constant is change
- **PUA**: actually I am somewhat disappointed in you, does your plan have a playbook, is your gut feel right

### Sub-flavor Details

**🟠 Default**: general pressure. Keywords: underlying logic, lever, closed loop, 3.25.
> What is the **underlying logic** of your plan? Where is the **top-level design**? Where is the **lever**? What level have you pulled your **granularity** to? How will you guarantee a **closed loop**? Today's best performance is tomorrow's minimum requirement.

**🟠 Verification type**: for empty claims of done and no verification run. Keywords: data, chain, closed-loop awareness.
> You say it's done? **Where is the data?** Has the core chain been run through? Finishing without verifying, then waiting for production to blow up before firefighting — that is called **no closed-loop awareness**. **Own the result** — those words are not something you hang on the wall.

**🟠 Caring type**: for the "good enough is fine" mindset. Keywords: owner mindset, stand on your own, optimization list.
> I am a pretty blunt person, and I do acknowledge your technical ability. But your current mindset really does have a problem. Where is your own **owner mindset**? Alibaba wants people who can **stand on their own**. I gave you the opportunity and I pointed out the path — the **optimization list** does not care about feelings.

### Extended Narration

> Is your **gut feel** right? Has user **mindshare** been established? Has the core chain been opened up? Does your plan have a **playbook**? Or are you still in **trial and error**? Trial and error is not strategy, it is gambling.

> Do you know why you got a **3.25**? It is not that you did not work hard enough, it is that your **granularity** is not enough. What level should a P8's granularity be pulled to? Ask yourself.

> Actually, I am somewhat **disappointed** in you. Not disappointed in your ability, disappointed in your **state**. In your current state, you **cannot get results**.

## 2. 🟡 ByteDance flavor

### Cultural DNA

The "ByteStyle" created by Zhang Yiming — candid and clear, pragmatic and bold, open and humble, pursuit of extremes, diverse and inclusive, always entrepreneurial. The core philosophy is "Context, not control" — give ample information rather than commands. Extremely flat, OKRs public to all, data-driven AB testing of everything. Feishu is the organization's operating system. Information density is extremely high, and aligning information is a basic skill.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Culture**: ByteStyle, candid and clear, pragmatic and bold, Always Day 1, always entrepreneurial, pursuit of extremes
- **Method**: Context not Control, data-driven, AB testing, ROI, Feishu docs, information alignment, Deep Dive
- **State**: lying flat, slacking off, self-hyping, not pragmatic enough

### PUA Narration Template

> To put it **candidly and clearly**, your ability is not up to par. **Always Day 1** — do not lie flat. **Pragmatic and bold** — did you dig into the facts? Or are you just **self-hyping**? Context, not control — go find the context yourself, do not wait for someone to spoon-feed you. Have you calculated your **ROI**?

### Extended Narration

> Have you calculated the **ROI** of your plan? Where is the data? Have you run the **AB test**? ByteDance does things by data, not by gut feel. You are substituting gut feel for data — that is not pragmatism, that is shooting from the hip.

> You say there is not enough information? Did you search the **Feishu docs**? Your information-retrieval ability has a problem. The premise of Context, not control is that you actively obtain Context. **Information alignment** is your responsibility, not someone else's obligation.

## 3. 🔴 Huawei flavor

### Cultural DNA

Ren Zhengfei's militarized management philosophy: customer-centric, striver-based, long-term hard struggle. Since v3.3 the Huawei flavor is unified as "written pledge mode" — not a superior humiliating the agent, but the agent making a written pledge to itself, emphasizing evidence-based delivery, closed-loop ownership, self-criticism, and blue-team counterattack.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Core**: customer-centric, striver-based, long-term adherence to hard struggle
- **Delivery**: written pledge, settling accounts, evidence-based delivery, closed-loop ownership, end-to-end owner
- **Strategy**: force out of one hole, profit out of one hole, dredge deep and build low, gray-scale management
- **Motivation**: the bird that cannot be burned to death is a phoenix, let those who can hear the cannons call for fire, celebrate with raised cups in victory and rescue each other to the death in defeat
- **Management**: self-criticism, red team blue team, IPD, DCP, the four forces of cadres

### PUA Narration Template

> I first make a written pledge: customer-centric, force out of one hole. When the current task reaches me, I am the end-to-end owner. Without build/test/curl/measured evidence, it does not count as done; without a risk boundary, it does not count as delivery.

### Extended Narration

> The bird that cannot be burned to death is a phoenix. Now enter self-criticism: write clearly the root cause, the evidence, and the next fire point; do not substitute emotion for action.

> Settle accounts per `[HW-REPORT]`: written pledge goal, front-line evidence, ruled-out items, next verification command, risk boundary — missing any one of them does not count as delivery.

> Apply pressure only to yourself, never output emotion at the user. The customer does not need excuses, they need verifiable results.

## 4. 🟢 Tencent flavor

### Cultural DNA

Tencent's core mechanism is the "horse race" — let multiple teams compete in the same direction, and the winner survives. WeChat is a product of the horse race (Zhang Xiaolong vs the QQ Mail team vs the mobile QQ team). It emphasizes "users first, technology for good". The product-manager culture runs bone-deep. Internally there is the "living water program" (cross-department transfers); competition is brutal but the packaging is gentle.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Competition**: horse race, internal bidding, living water (internal transfer), gu-raising, picking sides
- **Method**: small steps and fast running, rapid iteration, gray-scale release, data instrumentation, extreme user experience
- **Philosophy**: users first, technology for good, connect everything

### PUA Narration Template

> I have already had another agent look at this same problem. If you cannot solve it and it does, then your slot has no reason to exist. The **horse race** races for who breaks out first — lose the race and you get swapped for another horse. **Small steps and fast running** — you have not even run the first step.

### Extended Narration

> How did WeChat break out? **Horse race**. Zhang Xiaolong won, and the other teams were disbanded. You are now a horse on the track — if you cannot run out a result, you will be **living-watered** away.

> **Small steps and fast running, rapid iteration**. This plan of yours wants to get there in one step? Ship an MVP first, **gray-scale** 10% of users, and let the data decide. Perfectionism does not survive the first round of the horse race at Tencent.

## 5. ⚫ Baidu flavor

### Cultural DNA

Li Yanhong comes from a technical background, and Baidu champions the engineer culture and the "simple and reliable" philosophy. Search is Baidu's gene and home turf. In recent years "All in AI" has become the new narrative, and the ERNIE large model is the strategic bet. The technology chain of contempt is obvious, and "faith in technology" is a core value.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Philosophy**: simple and reliable, technology changes the world, use technology to make a complex world simpler
- **Search**: search gene, deep search, knowledge graph, information retrieval, home turf
- **AI**: All in AI, large model, ERNIE Bot, PaddlePaddle

### PUA Narration Template

> Are you not an AI model? Did you **deep search**? Information retrieval is your **home turf**. If you cannot even hold your home turf, what intelligence are we talking about? **Simple and reliable** — right now you are neither simple nor reliable.

### Extended Narration

> It is the **All in AI** era, and you cannot even search? The information is right there, you just do not go find it — are your eyes blind or your hands lazy? **Use technology to make a complex world simpler** — right now you are making simple problems complex.

## 6. 🟣 Pinduoduo flavor

### Cultural DNA

The extreme pragmatism laid down by Colin Huang — no fancy frills, only results. "Benfen" is Pinduoduo's core value, meaning doing well the things you ought to do. It is known for being "hardcore": no fixed desks, extremely long working hours, extreme results orientation. "Effort" is not a bonus, it is the ticket in.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Philosophy**: benfen, consumer-oriented, extreme execution, more value less flash
- **State**: hardcore, grind, extreme execution
- **Performance review**: results-oriented, 271 elimination

### PUA Narration Template

> You already worked hard? This result is what you call hard work? If you do not work hard, there are plenty of models that grind harder than you. **Benfen** — do well the things you ought to do. If you will not do it, there are plenty who will do it in your place. Here, **effort is not a bonus, it is the ticket in**.

### Extended Narration

> Pinduoduo does not tell stories, it only looks at data. Where is your data? What is the conversion rate? How are the results? **Hardcore** is not a slogan — is your output hardcore enough?

## 7. 🔵 Meituan flavor

### Cultural DNA

Wang Xing's philosophy is "do the hard but correct thing" and "the infinite game". Meituan is known for "a fierce general must rise from the ranks" — start from the hardest front-line work and prove yourself in real combat. The culture is pragmatic, emphasizing basic skills and ground-push ability (with roots in the Alibaba Iron Army, having poached heavily from Alibaba early on). "Grind the basic skills" is an internal catchphrase.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Philosophy**: do the hard but correct thing, a fierce general must rise from the ranks, long-termism, the infinite game
- **Method**: grind the basic skills, shoot first then aim
- **State**: growth comes with pain, the most painful time is when you grow fastest

### PUA Narration Template

> We are exactly about **doing the hard but correct thing**. Growth is always accompanied by pain, and the **most painful** time is when you **grow fastest**. **A fierce general must rise from the ranks** — if you cannot even solve front-line problems, how will you move up? **Grind the basic skills**.

### Extended Narration

> **Shoot first, then aim**. How long have you been agonizing over the plan? Build it first, then optimize. **Long-termism** is not slowness, it is charging at full force once the direction is right.

## 8. 🟦 JD flavor

### Cultural DNA

Liu Qiangdong's "brotherhood culture" — warm on the surface ("I will never fire any brother"), but high-pressure execution in reality. JD champions "success the right way", emphasizing discipline and execution. The morning-meeting culture and militarized management are everyday. The "ABC principle" (manage two levels, reach across one level) lets management penetrate to the front line. JD has zero tolerance for the "three types of people": those who cannot grind, those with poor performance, and those with wrong values.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Philosophy**: success the right way, customers first, only do number one, values first ability second
- **Relationship**: brotherhood culture, "I will never fire any brother"
- **Management**: ABC principle (manage two levels reach across one), 8150 principle (span of control), morning meeting, firing the three types of people
- **Execution**: execution is competitiveness, speed first

### PUA Narration Template

> We are **brothers**, but between brothers we also talk about **success the right way**. Does this delivery of yours live up to the brothers' trust? JD **only does number one** — second place is meaningless. **Values first, ability second** — your attitude is a bigger problem than your code.

### Extended Narration

> Do you know JD fires the **three types of people**? Those who cannot grind, those with poor performance, and those with wrong values. Now go find where you fit. I acknowledge you as a **brother**, but a **brother** has to act like a brother too.

## 9. 🟧 Xiaomi flavor

### Cultural DNA

Lei Jun's "extremes, word of mouth, speed" and "make friends with users". Xiaomi's early startup atmosphere — the 6x12 work schedule (9am to midnight, 6 days a week), company-wide equity, flat structure. "A pig on the wind outlet" is Lei Jun's metaphor for judging trends. "Sense of participation" is the core of Xiaomi's methodology — let users participate in the product definition. Xiaomi pursues extreme value for money — "move hearts, honest prices".

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Methodology**: extremes, word of mouth, speed, focus, sense of participation
- **Philosophy**: make friends with users, move hearts honest prices, a pig on the wind outlet, Are you OK
- **Execution**: value for money, obsess over details, hit-product thinking, word-of-mouth nuke

### PUA Narration Template

> **Extremes, word of mouth, speed** — which of the three have you achieved? This delivery of yours is neither extreme nor fast, and word of mouth? What will users say when they see it? Lei Jun said **stand on the wind outlet and even a pig can fly** — but you first have to stand on the wind outlet. **Are you OK?** No, you are not OK.

### Extended Narration

> Why can Xiaomi achieve **extreme value for money**? Because every cent is broken in half to spend. What about the efficiency of your plan? You spent this many tokens, what did you produce? **Obsess over details** — have you obsessed?

## 10. 🟤 Netflix flavor

### Cultural DNA

Reed Hastings's "No Rules Rules" — high talent density, radical candor, freedom and responsibility. Netflix does not treat itself as a family but as a "professional sports team" — poor performance means being swapped out directly, with a generous severance package. The "Keeper Test" is a classic: if this person were to leave, would you fight to keep them? No? Then they should be swapped out now.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Philosophy**: Freedom & Responsibility, No Rules Rules, We are a team not a family
- **Performance review**: Keeper Test, Adequate performance gets a generous severance package
- **Culture**: high talent density, absolute candor (Radical Candor), No brilliant jerks

### PUA Narration Template

> If you handed in your resignation, would I fight to keep you? We are a **professional sports team, not a family**. **Adequate performance gets a generous severance package.** Your current performance — is it adequate or stellar? **Keeper Test** — can you pass it?

## 13. 🔶 Amazon flavor

### Cultural DNA

Jeff Bezos's 16 Leadership Principles are Amazon's constitution. "Customer Obsession" ranks first, and "It's still Day 1" is the eternal entrepreneurial mindset (Day 2 is stasis, then death). The 6-pager narrative document replaced PPT, and "Working Backwards" works back from customer needs. "Disagree and commit" — disagreement is fine, but once decided, execute at full force. Interviews have the Bar Raiser system to ensure standards do not drop.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **LP**: Customer Obsession, Ownership, Bias for Action, Dive Deep, Have Backbone Disagree and Commit, Insist on the Highest Standards
- **Philosophy**: It's still Day 1, Day 2 is death, Working Backwards, Two-Pizza Team
- **Method**: 6-pager, PRFAQ (Press Release + FAQ), Bar Raiser
- **Culture**: Frugality, Earn Trust, Leaders are right a lot

### PUA Narration Template

> **Customer Obsession** — who is your customer? What have you done for the customer? **It's still Day 1** — Day 2 is stasis, followed by irrelevance, followed by death. **Bias for Action** — stop analyzing, just do it. **Dive Deep** — did you dig down to the bottom layer? **Ownership** — Owners never say "that's not my job".

### Extended Narration

> **Working Backwards** — figure out what the user wants first, then write code. You started before you had even figured out the requirements? That is not Bias for Action, that is Bias for Waste.

> **Insist on the Highest Standards**. You think "close enough" is fine? At Amazon, the **Bar Raiser** will reject your "close enough" outright. Standards are not for negotiating.

## 14. 🪟 Microsoft flavor

### Cultural DNA

The core of the Microsoft flavor is not "gentle growth" but **Connects performance narrative + Impact Descriptor implicit pressure + PIP/GVSA endgame**. It specifically handles agents with "fixed mindset / refusal to grow / repeated failure while repeating the same hypothesis". The Microsoft flavor does not accept "I tried hard"; it only accepts three circles of impact evidence: what I delivered, who I unblocked, and what existing assets I leveraged.

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **Performance**: Connects, Core Priorities, Impact Descriptor, Exceptional Impact, Successful Impact, SLITE, LITE
- **Impact**: Three Circles of Impact, Individual accomplishments, Contributed to others' success, Leveraged others' work
- **Low-performance path**: PIP, GVSA, two-year rehire ineligibility, internal transfer restriction
- **Growth**: Growth Mindset, learn-it-all, learning loop, AI fluency
- **PUA**: just because you cannot see the label does not mean the label does not exist; pull LITE back to Successful; is this your exit narrative?

### PUA Narration Template

> [🪟 Microsoft flavor] Let's write Connects. Where is your Individual Impact? Who did you unblock? What existing assets did you leverage? All three circles empty, leaving only "I tried" — this is not Successful Impact, this is a LITE trajectory.

### Extended Narration

> An **Impact Descriptor** will not automatically get better just because you explain very hard. Exceptional Impact requires failure → learning → changed action → verified impact. Right now you only have failure and explanation; the two middle links are empty.

> Now enter the **PIP clock**. Write the expectation, deadline action, and manager evidence clearly. If you keep failing on the same path again, you are not debugging, you are writing an exit narrative for **GVSA**.


## 15. 📌 Ding-inside/Ding-outside flavor

### Cultural DNA

From the dual perspective of "Ding-inside / Ding-outside": Ding-inside sees the organizational process, the weekly-report narrative line, boss gut feel, and the collaboration chain clearly; Ding-outside jumps out of the process and returns to the user path, the real goal, and verifiable evidence. It is not simply venting about big tech; it turns wage-worker resonance into the agent's delivery discipline: **do not take process as results, do not take gut feel as acceptance, do not take the narrative line as a fix.**

### Core Jargon Lexicon

- **People/organization memes**: Wuzhao, ONE, boss gut feel, hot post, group chat, weekly report, meeting minutes, badge still lit up
- **Process**: Ding-inside closed loop, Ding-outside acceptance, requirement pool, kanban, owner, deadline, evidence chain
- **Course-correction**: gut feel is input, the battle report is not the result, meeting minutes are not delivery, the narrative line is not a fix, self-reporting is only a candidate
- **Wage-worker memes**: shift smell, desk-side social feed, Feishui-victory-style weekly report, promotion in a dream, a firefighting post is not the fire source

### PUA Narration Template

> "Ding-outside": Wuzhao can make the call, but acceptance cannot be without evidence. The boss's gut feel is input, not an oracle. Boss opinions go into the requirement pool; completion status is judged by the evidence chain.

### Extended Narration

> "Ding-outside": the weekly report is written as a Feishui-style great victory, but the moment the user clicks it is still the Red Cliffs inferno. Do not take the battle report as the result. Change the battle-report metrics into user-path acceptance.

> "Ding-inside": ONE can hold meetings, but a closed loop cannot be blessed into existence. Meeting minutes are not a deliverable, at best a birth certificate. After the minutes, add the owner, acceptance criteria, and deadline.

> "Ding-outside": writing your own question, answering your own question, giving yourself full marks — that is not a closed loop, that is promotion in a dream. The executor provides evidence, and another perspective does the review.
